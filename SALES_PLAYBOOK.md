# MCR — Contractor Acquisition & Sales Playbook
**Missed-Call Revenue Recovery for Local Service Businesses**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Local home-service contractors (plumbers, HVAC techs, electricians, roofers, garage door pros) don't have a lead generation problem; **they have a lead retention problem**.

* **The Brutal Reality**: 82% of homeowners who experience an emergency or need urgent repairs **hang up on voicemail and call the next listing on Google**.
* **The Math**: The average residential service ticket is **$450 to $1,800**. If a contractor misses just **5 calls a month**, they are bleeding **$2,250 to $9,000 in pure top-line revenue**.
* **The Solution (MCR)**: When a call rings out or is declined on the job site, MCR automatically triggers a personalized SMS text-back in **<60 seconds**, gathers customer trade details, collects damage photos, and notifies the contractor instantly with a ready-to-book **Job Card**.

---

## 2. The "Missed-Call Audit" Cold Outreach Strategy

### The 1:15 PM Secret Call Method
1. Search Google Maps for local service businesses in your target city (e.g., *"plumbers in Austin TX"* or *"HVAC repair Dallas"*).
2. Call their listed phone number between **1:00 PM and 2:30 PM** (peak job-site wrench-turning hours).
3. Do **not** leave a voicemail when it rings out.
4. Wait 3 minutes to see if they text back. 95% will not.
5. Send the contractor this text message from your cell phone (or call them back 10 minutes later):

```text
"Hey [Owner First Name], I actually just called your business 5 minutes ago to ask about a [water heater repair / AC service / panel upgrade] and got your voicemail. 

I'm not a homeowner—my name is [Your Name] and I live here in [City]. I run a service called MCR that catches missed calls for local trades. 

If I had been a real customer with water leaking through my ceiling, I would have already hired the next guy on Google and you would have lost an $800+ job. 

I want to set up our missed-call text recovery on your phone for free for 14 days to recover at least 3 jobs for you this month. Takes 2 minutes to turn on. Got 2 minutes to test it?"
```

> **Owner**: "Uh, yeah, my phone was ringing while I was pulling a line. Who is this?"

> 
> We built a dead-simple tool called MCR for local trades. The second you miss a call on a job site, it automatically texts the homeowner within 30 seconds from your company name: *'Hey, this is Mike at Apex Plumbing. Sorry I missed your call, are you having an emergency?'*
> 
> It asks them what's wrong, collects pictures of the issue, and drops a booked lead into your phone before they can call your competitor. 
> 
> I'm putting 10 local contractors on a free 14-day pilot. No credit card, no contract. You literally just dial one carrier code on your phone right now. Want to see how it works?"

## 5. Live Setup Walkthrough (Carrier Forwarding on Call)

Stay on the phone with the contractor and guide them through their carrier code:

1. **AT&T**: Guide them to dial `*61*[MCR_ASSIGNED_NUMBER]#` and press call.
2. **Verizon**: Guide them to dial `*71[MCR_ASSIGNED_NUMBER]` and press call.
3. **T-Mobile**: Guide them to dial `**61*[MCR_ASSIGNED_NUMBER]**20#` and press call.
4. **The Live Proof Call**:
   * Have them hang up.
   * Immediately call their cell phone from an alternate line and let it ring 4 times.
   * Have them watch their phone.
   * Within 25 seconds, their test phone buzzes with the automatic qualification text.
   * **Reaction**: Every contractor says: *"Damn, that was fast."* — The deal is 90% closed right there.
